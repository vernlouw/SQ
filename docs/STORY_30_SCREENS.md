# Mopocalypse Now: the thirty-screen adventure

This expands the sixteen story beats in the supplied `Space_Quest_VII_Codex_Handoff.md` into **thirty distinct playable locations**. The handoff reconstructs the earlier conversation; it is an outline, rather than a complete verbatim screenplay. The room dialogue and puzzle steps below develop that outline into a connected adventure.

A screen means a room Roger can enter, walk around, inspect and interact with. Title screens, sound settings, flight transitions, dialogue windows, the burger order window and the ending do not increase this count. The four existing locations remain part of the adventure, alongside twenty-six new rooms.

## Story

R0-GER, Roger Wilco's clone, has stolen the **Mop of Destiny**. He intends to combine it with five other legendary cleaning artifacts and sanitize the entire universe. His definition of dirt includes uncertainty, individuality and Roger's employment record.

Roger follows a museum star map to Labion, then travels through Monolith Burger, Plexi-Prime, StarCon Academy, Polysorbate LX and Glitzon. He collects the remaining artifacts to keep them away from his clone. At the final confrontation, an automated inspection system confiscates his collection and delivers it to R0-GER's apparatus. Roger's most useful possession turns out to be something no respectable cleaning machine would accept: Monolith Burger fryer sludge.

## Artifact continuity

| Artifact | Where Roger finds it | Story role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mop of Destiny | R0-GER's apparatus in the final chamber | Stolen before Roger's investigation begins. The museum's ordinary maintenance mop is a separate tool. |
| Bucket of Eternal Suds | Labion shrine | First legendary artifact Roger recovers. |
| Squeegee of Power | Plexi-Prime vault | Protected by the reflective-panel puzzle. |
| Vacuum of Infinite Suction | StarCon laboratory | Requires academy training authorization. |
| Broom of Cosmic Sweep | Polysorbate LX alley | Obtained through the market trade. |
| Polish of Eternal Shine | Glitzon vault | Recovered after dealing with the dangerous glare. |

Roger's collection remains distinct from ordinary puzzle items. When the clone's intake activates, it takes only these five artifacts. Sludge sabotage makes the stolen Mop of Destiny reachable; the final shutdown returns the other five. There is no need to spend an artifact in an earlier puzzle or to lose an irreplaceable item during a comic failure.

The main story has **300 points**: the original opening's 100 plus 200 across the expanded campaign. Optional inspections, the souvenir badge and the restaurant's relief shift are independent of that score. Each solved story step scores once.

## Complete puzzle walkthrough

This describes the successful route. Inspecting objects or talking to characters along the way supplies the same clues within the game.

### Opening: 100 points

1. In the diner, inspect the news screen and talk to Bex for the service chit and grease permission.
2. Pick up the grease jar. Use it on the maintenance panel, then enter Service Dock 7.
3. Give the service chit to Voss for the keycard and maintenance pass.
4. Use the keycard on the locker. Pick up the released service mop, then use the keycard on the terminal to authorize the museum lift.
5. Enter the museum. Show the maintenance pass to the guardian to enable cleaning mode.
6. Combine the ion cleaner with the service mop. Use the charged mop on the spill, then pick up the star map from the plinth. This uses the ordinary maintenance equipment; it is not the legendary Mop of Destiny.
7. Return to the dock, use the star map on the terminal and select Labion at the shuttle.

Monolith Burger can be visited before step 7. Its dining-room quiz has three tickets and rewards one redeemable staff-meal coupon. This is optional, and its reward is separate from the kitchen's story items.

### Labion: 35 points

8. At the landing pad, pick up the empty flask. Keep it for the kitchen's grease trap later.
9. Enter the jungle and pick up the vine rope.
10. At the bog, use the vine rope on the quicksand crossing to obtain the bog herb. Reaching into the hazard without the proper tool produces a comic failure with Retry.
11. At the shrine checkpoint, use the bog herb on the guard to open the route.
12. In the shrine, use the maintenance pass on the custodian oath pedestal to release its seal.
13. Pick up the Bucket of Eternal Suds. Return to the landing pad and fly to Monolith Burger.

### Monolith Burger: 35 points

14. From the dining room, take the shuttle berth door into the service berth and operate its grease valve.
15. Take the berth's left door back to the restaurant, then use the staff kitchen hatch. Use the empty flask on the kitchen grease trap to collect fryer sludge.
16. Talk to the kitchen cook to receive the service token.
17. In the freezer, use the service token on the staff records icebox to obtain the thawed employee memo.
18. In the arcade, use the employee memo on the Astro Chicken machine to obtain the arcade code.
19. Talk to the arcade attendant with the arcade code, fryer sludge and Bucket of Eternal Suds in your pockets. This unlocks the Plexi-Prime shuttle route. Take the arcade's right door to the berth and board the shuttle for Plexi-Prime. Keep the sludge for the finale.

### Plexi-Prime: 35 points

20. Land at the dock and walk through the plaza. Pick up the mirror wrench.
21. In the reflection gallery, use the wrench on the panel mechanism to align the mirrors.
22. In beam control, show the maintenance pass to the controller.
23. Operate the console with the mirrors aligned and the controller's authorization.
24. Enter the vault and pick up the Squeegee of Power. Return to the dock and select StarCon Academy.

### StarCon Academy: 35 points

25. Walk from the dock into the registration hall. Talk to registration staff for the academy badge.
26. Take the classroom doorway on the hall's left. Use the academy badge on the lesson console to earn the mop certificate.
27. In the simulator, use the mop certificate on the exercise console to pass its practical test.
28. In the laboratory, show the mop certificate to the scientist for collection authorization.
29. Pick up the Vacuum of Infinite Suction. Return to the academy dock and fly to Polysorbate LX.

### Polysorbate LX: 20 points

30. Walk from the dock to the market and show the vendor the mop certificate to obtain a trade chit.
31. In the alley, use the trade chit on the cabinet to open it.
32. Collect the red sealed artifact case on the alley's left to obtain the Broom of Cosmic Sweep. The same collection supplies complimentary sunglasses and unlocks Glitzon. The custodian's visible broom is an ordinary tool. Return to the dock and fly there.

### Glitzon: 20 points

33. Walk from the dock to the boulevard. Use the sunglasses on the dazzling vault entrance scanner.
34. Talk to the attendant after shielding the scanner to obtain vault clearance.
35. Enter the vault and pick up the Polish of Eternal Shine. This supplies the route to R0-GER's chamber.

### R0-GER's chamber: 20 points

36. With the Bucket, Squeegee, Vacuum, Broom and Polish collected, operate the artifact intake. The clone confiscates those five artifacts once and arms his cleaning machine.
37. Use the fryer sludge on the apparatus. Its clean-room logic cannot cope with the contamination; the stolen mop becomes reachable.
38. Pick up the Mop of Destiny.
39. Operate the apparatus again with no pocket item selected. This returns the five confiscated artifacts, stops the universal wipe and completes the 300-point story.

Returning to a solved room never resets its progress. If Roger reaches a locked route early, its character or console explains the missing requirement. The five artifacts and fryer sludge form the required final combination; optional burger coupons and souvenir jokes remain optional.

## Chapter structure

### The Mop Job: diner, dock and museum

The existing opening establishes the investigation. Bex gives Roger a service chit and permission to take the grease jar. Grease repairs the hatch; the chit obtains dock credentials; credentials release the maintenance equipment and authorize the museum lift. The guardian's cleaning mode, cleaner and ordinary mop clear the spill. Roger recovers the star map and programs it at the dock terminal.

The shuttle now takes Roger to the next playable planet. The restaurant remains available as an optional early excursion. The museum map is the route to Labion, rather than the end of the adventure.

### Labion: five screens

The landing pad establishes the shrine expedition and supplies the empty flask. Jungle vines, the bog and the checkpoint provide a connected obstacle sequence before the shrine. Roger's maintenance pass opens the altar seal; he collects the Bucket of Eternal Suds and learns that the trail leads through Monolith Burger. The return walk and shuttle stay available; returning to an earlier room does not reset its solved puzzles.

### Monolith Burger: dining room and four service screens

The dining room's three-order relief shift remains an optional joke and reward. The main investigation instead uses the service berth, kitchen, freezer and arcade. These are separate navigable rooms. The service valve enables the grease trap; the flask collects its sludge. Kitchen work supplies the service token, and the freezer memo explains how to recover the Plexi-Prime travel code from the arcade. The attendant authorizes that route once Roger has the code, bucket and sludge. Completing or redeeming the optional coupon is never required to finish the story.

### Plexi-Prime: five screens

The dock and plaza introduce the facility. Roger finds the wrench to align the gallery's mirror array, then shows his maintenance pass and operates the control-room beam circuit. The vault contains the Squeegee of Power and the StarCon lead. The console explains either missing prerequisite; a wrong tool is retained.

### StarCon Academy: five screens

The dock leads to registration, the academy badge enables the class and its mop certificate enables the simulator. Roger also shows the certificate to the laboratory scientist before collecting the vacuum. The academy turns Roger's janitorial skills into an absurdly formal certification process. There is no requirement to achieve a score in the optional Monolith Burger job.

### Polysorbate LX: three screens

The dock introduces the market. Showing the mop certificate to the vendor obtains the trade chit, and the alley's cabinet accepts it. Roger receives the Broom of Cosmic Sweep, complimentary protective eyewear and the Glitzon lead. The correct trade consumes only the agreed ordinary item; the legendary collection stays intact.

### Glitzon and the finale: four screens

Protective eyewear makes the boulevard's entrance scanner usable, and its attendant clears Roger for the vault. Roger collects the Polish of Eternal Shine and discovers R0-GER's chamber. With all five artifacts recovered, the clone's intake executes once. Fryer sludge contaminates the maintenance circuit. Roger retrieves the stolen Mop of Destiny and operates the apparatus again, defeating the universal cleaning plan and recovering his collection.

## Interaction and continuity rules

- Clicking floor space walks Roger there. Clicking a person approaches and talks; clicking an object approaches and performs its contextual action. Talk applies to characters.
- Each screen supplies story interactions, optional inspections and a way back or onward. A flight, popup or fullscreen joke does not count as a room.
- Solved puzzles, inventory, room visits and optional burger progress survive travel and saving. Older diner, dock, museum and restaurant checkpoints remain usable.
- Wrong inventory combinations give a response without consuming valuable items. Failed training or comic hazards can be retried; they do not create permanent dead ends.
- Return routes remain available after an artifact is collected. New destinations unlock as their preceding chapter is solved.
- The final intake happens once. Repeating an interaction cannot duplicate, destroy or permanently withhold a legendary artifact.
- Historical callbacks are optional context. A player can solve the main story from its own dialogue and clues without having played SQ1–6.
